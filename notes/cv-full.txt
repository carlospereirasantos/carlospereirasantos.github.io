Professional experience:
Since 2009 Breda University of Applied Sciences
Professor Applied Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (since Jan 2025)
Carlos leads applied Data Science and AI research at BUas, with a focus on data-driven real-time
systems, AI models, game engine technology, and distributed computing. He shapes the
university's AI policy, infrastructure, and laboratory strategy, and leads applied AI research
initiatives aligned with BUas's strategic vision, including the AI Pioneer Team, IT Server Compute
Infrastructure and Game Hub.
Cradle Research Lab Coordinator & Lead Researcher (2015 — 2025)
Carlos founded, built, and led Cradle, BUas's applied research lab focused on Games, Media and
AI, growing it from an initial concept to a team of 25 researchers (23.0 FTE) delivering over 50
applied R&D projects across games, XR, AI, virtual production, and serious games.
He was responsible for team design, recruitment, professional development, and budget
management, culminating in Cradle becoming the only R&D group at a Dutch University of
Applied Sciences to receive an all-excellent accreditation in 2024.
Contributed to the design and development of the Applied Data Science & AI (ADS&AI) BSc
programme. Designed, and established key BUas lab facilities: XR Stage (Virtual Production
Studio), XR Lab, Motion Capture Studio, Photogrammetry Studio, and AI Makerspace, available
to research, education and industry. Founded and organised annually since 2020 the Virtual
Production Gathering, a lead conference attracting 200+ industry participants.
Senior Lecturer (2009 — 2015)
Carlos joined BUas as a lecturer responsible for core technical and creative modules across
bachelor and master programmes, establishing himself as a key contributor to curriculum
development in game technology, AI, and interactive media.
Responsible Lecturer for: Artificial Intelligence, Game Networking, Mobile Development, Network
Programming, and Experimental Games. Responsible Teacher for the Technology module of the
Master Media Innovation (MMI), which he subsequently transitioned into the Master Game
Technology (MGT) programme.
2001-2009 GMV / Skysoft Portugal, SA
Technical Director: Aeronautics, Security and Defence Department
Carlos led the software design and development of technical solutions for Critical Safety Software
like RTMC-OSPF and RTMC-IPF Subsystems of Galileo (the European GPS), promoted by the
European Space Agency.
Design authority and lead developer on complex network server component, of Naval radar and
communication system, deployed in the islands of Azores, Madeira and Canaries. Lead developer
of a propeller balancing software for Socata TB-30 Epsilon airplanes for the Portuguese Air Force
to improve the vibration reduction.
Lead Programmer on a 3D Aircraft Simulator, development and integration with a Radar Air
Traffic Controller Simulator, which is used mainly for demonstrations.
